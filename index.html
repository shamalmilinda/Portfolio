02 ABOUT

Built around visual craft.

I am a creative 3D Artist and Digital Creator with experience in 3D Modeling, 3D Animation, Unreal Engine, Visual Design, and Digital Media. I enjoy creating detailed 3D assets, animations, environments, and visually engaging digital content.

I have worked on various 3D modeling and animation projects, as well as designing 3D wallpapers and developing visual concepts. I also have experience working with Unreal Engine for real-time 3D and game development.

In addition to my visual work, I have experience creating 8D Audio, combining visual creativity with audio production.

I also developed ANTIVISUAL ENGINE, an AI-powered visual generation engine designed to create AI-generated images and explore new possibilities in digital creativity and visual technology.

My work brings together 3D Art, Animation, Unreal Engine, Visual Design, Audio, and AI Technology, with a strong interest in exploring innovative ideas and creating new digital experiences.
